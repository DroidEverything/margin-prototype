MARGIN / INDEPENDENT NASA SPACE APPS CHALLENGE PROTOTYPE

MISSION CONTROL UI SYSTEM

A restrained aerospace interface derived from NASA's documented visual language: precise typography, disciplined color, grids, hairlines, technical annotation, telemetry, and high-signal state treatment. This document is the reference contract for the frontend.

08 / Model direction

PBR / GLB / REV 01

MARGIN Mission Bus

The current prototype uses a lightweight Canvas2D preview rendered from an original PBR GLB. The GLB is shipped separately in /models so the future Three.js Build Bay can load the same asset without redesigning the interface.

For drop-in real-world models, preferred repositories researched for this project include NASA 3D Resources, NASA's Sketchfab profile, and Smithsonian Open Access. Their source URLs and licensing notes are in MODEL_SOURCES.md.

10 / Brand safety

Do not place the NASA Meatball, Worm, or Seal in the MARGIN logo, title bar, or custom brand mark. The prototype uses its own MARGIN wordmark and an explicit “independent prototype” note. NASA's current Brand Guidelines say these identifiers are protected and may not be used to imply endorsement without permission.
